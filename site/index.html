Writers Block

A songwriting assistant for iPhone, iPad and Android.

Writers Block is a native-first songwriting assistant. It is a lyrics editor with rhyme and prosody tools, on-device AI suggestions for the next line, voice memos for melodies and takes, and optional backup of your songs to Google Drive or iCloud. Your songs live on your device; nothing is sent anywhere unless you turn a feature on.

  • Lyrics editor with sections and rhyme scheme markers
  • Rhyme, syllable and prosody tools built on a pronunciation dictionary
  • Next-line suggestions from a model that runs on your device
  • Voice memos attached to each song
  • Backup to your own Google Drive or iCloud, and a plain ZIP export
